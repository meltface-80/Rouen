## Rouen + HQPWeb — v2.0.0-beta2 - Your Android phone as a Roon zone, at home and away

A music-discovery companion, remote and wall display for [Roon](https://roon.app), with
[hqpweb](https://github.com/statelycurmudgeon/hqpweb) built in for HQPlayer. New in v2.0.0 (beta): **Rouen for
Android**, which makes your phone a Roon zone at home and away from home, with all the music still coming through
Roon.

**📖 Everything is on the guide: https://meltface-80.github.io/Rouen**

- [Features](https://meltface-80.github.io/Rouen/#features)
- [Install with Docker](https://meltface-80.github.io/Rouen/#install) (Linux, a NAS, macOS) ·
  [Windows](https://meltface-80.github.io/Rouen/#windows) · [Unraid](https://meltface-80.github.io/Rouen/#unraid)
- [Rouen for Android](https://meltface-80.github.io/Rouen/#android): setting it up, using it, what it doesn't do, and
  why the music might drop out
- [Rouen + HQPWeb](https://meltface-80.github.io/Rouen/#hqpweb): HQPlayer control, from statelycurmudgeon's hqpweb,
  with thanks, under its MIT licence
- [Uninstall](https://meltface-80.github.io/Rouen/#uninstall)

Downloads (the Windows file and the Android app) are on the
[releases page](https://github.com/meltface-80/Rouen/releases). What changed in each version is in
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
