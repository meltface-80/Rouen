Features

Everything talks to your existing Roon Core through the official extension API — or, with the optional native mode, as Roon's own apps do — and the app never changes your library files. An optional read-only /music mount adds file-tag labels, release dates, local waveforms, the local-files badge and the format filters. Tap i on any feature for how to set it up and use it.

📱Rouen for AndroidNew

⬇ Rouen for AndroidRouen for Android makes your phone a Roon zone, with Rouen on your server or PC.

New in v2.0: the Rouen app makes your Android phone a Roon zone, at home and away from home. All the music still comes from Roon. Rouen carries its sound to the phone as Opus 256 or lossless, through Tailscale built into Rouen when you are out. There's no second server and no extra streaming login. Also: downloads for offline listening, and Android Auto.

How to set it up and use it. See Rouen for Android below: setting it up, using it, what it doesn't do, and why the music might drop out.

🪟Native on Windows

Since v1.9.67, Rouen runs on a Windows PC as one file — no Docker, no Node.js to install. Download Rouen-v2.0.8-windows-x64.exe from the release, run it, and it unpacks itself, opens Rouen in your browser and runs in its own window. It updates at the same time as every other install, by the same one tap, and your pairing, account and history are kept through every update.

How to set it up and use it. Rouen runs in a black window on your computer and you use it in your web browser. See Install on Windows below for simple step-by-step instructions.

🎛️Native mode

Rouen talks to your Roon Core the way Roon's own apps do, both ways. Your library is the Core's own album list. A heart in Rouen is Roon's favourite. Listen later is one list shared with Roon. Dynamic Playlists are Roon's smart playlists, with their covers, and playlists made in Rouen are Roon's own. Add to library uses Roon's own search, and play, pause, skip, seek, volume, mute, shuffle, repeat and Roon Radio go straight to the Core. New in v1.9.66: each zone's crossfade, volume levelling and sleep timer, its DSP in a window of its own (each item on or off; Headroom, Crossfeed and Sample rate conversion's settings; Parametric EQ bands), and Edit for any of Roon's smart playlists, in Rouen's Focus editor. A status board in Settings → Native mode shows a green, red or grey light for each, and why when one fails; the official extension API is the fallback for everything.

How to set it up and use it. Native mode is chosen once, at install or at the first start after updating to v1.9.65, and then stays on, with no switch: if its path fails, the official extension API takes over until it works again. Its status is under Settings → Native mode. Read the notice there first: native mode is unofficial, not made or supported by Roon Labs, and uses the private protocol Roon's own apps use — a Roon update can break it until Rouen is updated. Keep port 9332 on your own network. Rouen's own playlists, Listen later and Dynamic Playlists move into Roon by themselves; the status board says what moved and what couldn't, and why. Zone settings and DSP are under the bar's zone button ("Play on"), or the Shelf's zone menu: tap DSP for its window. A smart playlist is edited from its page under Dynamic Playlists; one the editor can't show whole, or an edit Roon can't hold, is refused with the reason.

🔭More around every album

Below the review in every album: More by the artist and the records they appear on, from your library, then Similar artists and Similar albums from Last.fm — three of each, with More for the rest. Last.fm is read-only: a free key, no sign-in, nothing scrobbled. Also new: Random Album asks before it plays (Play now, Play next or Queue), Random album radio doesn't play an album again within six months, and Smart Picks start on Nowhere — ask me rather than going straight into your library. Fixed in v1.9.2: with a genre filter on, an album opened from an artist's page no longer answers "Album not found".

How to set it up and use it. Open any album and scroll below the review. More by and Appears on need nothing; the Similar rows need a free Last.fm key under Settings → Setup → API Keys. A similar artist or album in your library opens in the app, the rest are marked Last.fm ↗ and open on Last.fm. Choose where Smart Picks go under Settings → Setup → Smart Picks → Send each day's picks to.

📚Shelf

Flick through your collection the way you once flicked through a rack of CDs or records — made for a tablet on a stand or a TV. A rack of spines, as tall as the screen, turns under your finger: a short swipe moves one album, swipe and hold keeps it turning, and a hard flick spins it to land on an album three seconds later. Each spine reads, from the top, the artist, the album and its version (Deluxe Edition, a remaster), and from the foot up its catalogue number, year and format (FLAC 24/96, MP3, Qobuz, TIDAL). Choose what is on it by Genre, Artist letter, Year, Label or Type, and file it in any Order; with nothing chosen it is the whole library, A → Z by artist, and it opens on the record that is playing. Along the bottom is a slim bar: play/pause, what is playing, where you are on the shelf, Spin, the zone and its volume.

The queue is a pane on the right — tap a track to play from there — and small tabs at the edges fold the choices away to the left and the queue to the right; with both away the shelf has the whole screen.

New in v1.9.68: tap the album in front and it turns into a full-screen album view: Play now and Queue under the cover, the album's review, more by the artist (tap one to go to it) and every track — hold a track to choose it, tap more to add them, and play just those. On a phone held upright the album in front is a tall card with its details under the cover. The catalogue numbers and kinds come from your files' tags, then Discogs, then — with native mode on — your Roon server.

New in v1.9.69: on a phone the choices open full screen from the tab at the top of the shelf, and Show N albums at their foot takes you back to the shelf; a tablet or a computer keeps the side pull-out. A phone's spines carry the catalogue number at their foot too.

New in v1.9.70: two more tabs: Formats, your albums by format and resolution ("24/96", "DSD64 2CH", "MP3"), and Favourites, the albums hearted in Roon. Genres can be sorted by count or A → Z, and with native mode on they come from your Roon server.

New in v1.9.71: on a desktop, a TV or a tablet on its side, the album you open from the Shelf is the library's three columns — the cover, its buttons and the credits at the left, About this album in the middle, the tracks with their lengths at the right.

How to set it up and use it. Nothing to switch on. Open ☰ → Shelf, tap Shelf › on the wall display, or point a browser at http://<server-ip>:3399/shelf. The first time, a popup shows the gestures; tick Don't show again to stop it reappearing after each update. Tap a genre, letter, decade, label or type to choose it and again to undo; once two are chosen in a section, Clear all × clears it. Spin (in the bar) does what a flick does, and a mouse wheel, a trackpad and — once the shelf has been clicked — the arrow keys turn it too. The bar's zone button plays somewhere else, and its speaker opens the volume. Tap the album in front for its album view; Back, × or Escape closes it. The fold-away tabs sit at mid-height on each side (on a tablet held upright the choices fold up, from a tab below them, and the queue is a drawer over the shelf; on a phone the choices cover the screen and Show N albums closes them); each device remembers which sides it left folded, and the queue reads nothing from Roon while it is folded. ‹ Remote (top left) goes back to the app, and Wall Display › (top right) shows while the wall display is switched on — and then, on a device with a screensaver time, Shelf goes to the wall display after that long untouched.

📺Remote ⇄ wall display

☰ → Wall display turns the remote into the wall display for its zone, and ‹ Remote in its top-left corner brings it back exactly as you left it — always on screen now, faint, and one tap: off-white over a dark screen, grey over a light artist photo. Shelf › sits opposite it, top right. A screensaver timer switches to the display after 1 to 60 minutes untouched — per device and off unless you choose it. It waits while Settings, a sheet or a selection is open.

How to set it up and use it. First switch on Settings → Wall Display → Wall display; the menu entry and the screensaver only appear while it is on. For the screensaver, on the device you want it on, pick a time under Settings → Wall Display → This device → Switch to the wall display. On the display, tap ‹ Remote to go back, or anywhere else to reveal the mode buttons.

🎚️HQPlayer control, from hqpweb

statelycurmudgeon's hqpweb, built in: a screen in the side menu for HQPlayer's 1x and Nx filters, its modulator or dither and presets, with Signalyst's ratings. Every change is read back; one that stops playback or that HQPlayer can't keep up with is put back by itself and remembered. A guide (beta) says where to start for your DAC, and Find your DAC looks it up. Several HQPlayers — found on your network or added by address — and several DACs behind one HQPlayer, each with its own answers, learned failures and presets.

How to set it up and use it. Switch on ☰ → Settings → HQPlayer → HQPlayer control, then Find HQPlayers and Add yours (or add it by address; the control port is 4321). Open ☰ → HQPlayer. The full steps are in Rouen + HQPWeb below.

💾Backup & restore

Back up your settings, playlists and Dynamic Playlists, Listen later, and API keys and sign-ins — any of the four. Backups are kept on the server (your last 10, plus 5 taken before a restore) and each can be downloaded. A restore keeps a copy of how things were first, restarts the server, and the page reloads by itself. A downloaded backup can be restored from the file.

How to set it up and use it. Open ☰ → Settings → Backup & restore, switch on the parts you want and tap Back up now. Each backup has Restore, Download and Delete; Restore from a file… takes one you downloaded. On an installed iPhone or iPad app, Download opens the share sheet — choose Save to Files. A backup with your API keys holds your sign-ins: keep it somewhere private.

⏭️Play next, everywhere

Play now, Play next, Queue on every track in the album view, and Play next in the selection menu for selected tracks and albums and in the album's ⋯ menu. What you choose plays straight after the current track — selected tracks in album order, selected albums in the order you picked them.

How to set it up and use it. Nothing to set up. Tap a track for its buttons, or long-press tracks or albums to select several (the one you pressed is already picked) and choose Play next.

📱Album view & mini player

On tablets and desktops About this album sits under the cover, beside the tracks, and on a tablet the album view fills the screen. The mini player is taller with bigger buttons on touch screens, and on a desktop it is a card in the corner that can be dragged anywhere. Now playing shows the whole cover, framed, and the seek bar is a brass level meter where a track has no waveform.

New in v1.9.70: on a landscape screen at least 1000×600 — a desktop, a TV, a tablet on its side — the album view is three columns: the cover, its buttons and the credits at the left, About this album in the middle, the tracks at the right, each scrolling on its own. With native mode on, each track shows its length. Every album's page lists its credits — who played, wrote and produced it, with pictures — and an artist page shows a band's current members.

New in v1.9.71: every album screen shows each track's length — the library's album view, the Shelf's and a TIDAL or Qobuz album's page — for albums your Roon server holds, with native mode on.

How to set it up and use it. Nothing to set up. On a desktop, drag the mini player by anything but a button; double-click it to send it back to its corner. Choose a theme under Settings → Setup → UI Settings → Theme.

🎛️UI Settings & label search

Make the app fit the screen it is on: text size for album and artist names and for each grid screen's title, a grid layout of Auto, 3 columns, 2 columns or List, and a tile size from −50% to +50% for album and label tiles everywhere. The Labels screen gains a search and a #–Z / Z–# order button, Home Screen rows drag smoothly into place, and a Smart Pick opens its album in your library or on your default streaming service.

How to set it up and use it. Open ☰ → Settings → Setup → UI Settings and pick from each list; it applies at once and is saved on that device only. On the Labels screen, tap the magnifying glass to search (× clears, then closes) and #–Z to reverse the order.

🐳A ready-made image

Install with one docker run — nothing to download or build — from ghcr.io/meltface-80/musicd-remote, built for x86 and 64-bit ARM: PCs and NAS boxes, a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 on a 64-bit OS, Apple Silicon Macs. Updates stay one tap in the app, and switching an existing install needs no reinstall: remove the old container and run the image with the same volume, and your Roon pairing and history carry over.

How to set it up and use it. Build your command with the configurator below and run it. In Roon, go to Settings → Extensions and click Enable on Rouen, then open http://<server-ip>:3399 on any device. The image and the data volume keep their musicd-remote names, so an existing install carries on unchanged.

🔄Your library, followed in seconds

Add albums in Roon and they are in the app within about a minute, with no rescan. While the app or the wall display is open it checks Roon every 30 seconds (every 3 minutes when nobody is looking) with three tiny calls that cost the Core almost nothing, follows a change until Roon settles, then reads the library once — so a big import costs one pass, not one per album. The side menu says when the library was last checked.

How to set it up and use it. Nothing to switch on. To force a fresh read, open the side menu (☰) and tap the Rescan library line at the bottom — it also shows the album count and when Roon was last checked.

🧭Simpler navigation

On a phone or tablet, Menu, first on the bar along the bottom, opens the side menu from every screen; a desktop keeps the ☰ at the top. The brass ‹ goes back to the album or screen you came from on an artist page (or a label opened from an album), Home anywhere else. A shorter side menu — Pitchfork, Labels, Qobuz, Tidal, Listen later, Discover, Dynamic Playlists, Playlists, Wall display, Shelf, HQPlayer, then Rescan library and Settings — with Random albums and Smart Picks opening from their Home rows and Import at the top of the Playlists screen. Settings is grouped as Native mode, Audio Devices, Wall Display, HQPlayer, Setup, Backup & restore and Updates; Setup holds Smart Picks, Record labels, Home Screen, UI Settings, Share Card, Discover and API Keys. On a phone every page opens full screen with its title pinned; on a tablet or desktop the list is a side panel.

New in v2.0.3: on a touch screen the bar along the bottom is Menu · Home · Library · Artists · Shelf · Search. Artists lists every album artist in your library, A to Z, each with a picture.

How to set it up and use it. Open the side menu (Menu on the bar on a phone or tablet, ☰ at the top on a desktop) and choose Settings at the bottom. Tap a row to open its page; the back arrow returns one level and × closes Settings. A setting with an ⓘ explains itself when you tap it. Switch the Random albums or Smart Picks row off under Settings → Setup → Home Screen and that screen comes back to the side menu.

🕒Listen later

Put an album aside to play another time — from its ⋯ menu, from a selection of albums on any wall, or with ＋ Listen later on a Smart Pick. Roon's own Listen later can't be reached from an extension, so this is the app's own list: kept on the server, the same on every device, and safe across a library rescan. Albums you own play straight from it; a Smart Pick that isn't in your library yet can be added in one tap or opened in Qobuz or TIDAL. It tidies itself: an album comes off once every track has been played through since you put it aside, on any zone, from any app.

How to set it up and use it. Choose Listen later from an album's ⋯ menu, or long-press albums on a wall to select several. Find the list under ☰ → Listen later or on its Home row (switch the row on or off and move it under Settings → Setup → Home Screen). To send each day's Smart Picks here, set Settings → Setup → Smart Picks → Send each day's picks to → Listen later.

↔️Previous & next album

Swipe the album card sideways, tap the chevrons on the cover's edges, or use the arrow keys to step to the albums either side — on the screen you opened it from, whether that's a Home row, the Library wall, or an artist or label page. Close the card and you land on the album you stepped to.

How to set it up and use it. Nothing to set up. Open an album from any row or wall, then swipe, tap ‹ › on the cover's edges, or press ← / →. An album opened from search or Now playing has no neighbours.

📡Live screens

Every screen keeps itself up to date. Home, the Library and Not played walls, the Queue, the album, artist and label pages, Smart Picks and Discover refresh themselves when something changes — a play, a release date found, a setting changed on another device. In place: your scroll position stays, nothing flashes “Loading…”, and nothing moves while your finger is on it.

How to set it up and use it. Nothing to switch on. Every open device checks the server every few seconds while it is on screen — so an album played on one phone leaves Album of the day on every other device too.

🧭Discover

New records by the artists you actually listen to. Read from your play history rather than your library, and ranked by how many separate days you played an act rather than by play count. Albums only, from the last two months, never a record you already have — and a remaster is refused only when the title says so and the act has an older release under the same name. Every row goes somewhere: in your Roon library it queues, otherwise it opens in your default streaming service. Uses Deezer's public catalogue and never touches your Roon Core.

How to set it up and use it. Switch on Settings → Setup → Discover → Discover (off by default) and pick the hour under Look for new records at (server time). It looks once a day; Refresh on the same page looks now. Then open ☰ → Discover. It works from your play history, so it gets better the more you listen. Your default streaming service is set under Settings → Setup → Share Card → Default.

📤Share card

Tap Share on any album or on Now playing and the record gets a card of its own — artwork, artist, title, release date and label, a description, and the Pitchfork score with its Best New Music flag where there is one. Underneath: where to hear it (Qobuz, TIDAL, Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Deezer, Bandcamp — and Qobuz opens the Qobuz app) and where to read about it (Wikipedia, Pitchfork and AllMusic for the album; Wikipedia and AllMusic for the artist if you want them). And “If you like this” suggests three acts worth hearing next, weighed by what you play — two you haven't heard near the acts you play most, and one you know with a record you don't own yet, each saying why. One in your Roon library is queued; one that isn't opens in your default service.

How to set it up and use it. Tap the share button in an album or on Now playing. Choose which services and review links appear, and the default service, under Settings → Setup → Share Card; holding a service button under the card also sets the default for that device.

〰️Waveform

The seek bar on Now playing and the wall display's progress strip draw the shape of the track you're listening to — where the quiet intro ends, where the loud middle is. Every bar is the level of its slice of the track, measured from both channels at full rate, and the shape sits under the playhead at every point in the track. Each track is analysed once and stored, and the next one in the queue is prepared while the current one plays. Works for local files and for Qobuz and TIDAL streams — Roon sends those straight to your endpoint and never to an extension, so the track is fetched from the service with your own account, turned into 4,000 loudness values and discarded. The audio you are listening to is never touched, no audio is written to disk, and Roon still handles all playback. Anything the app can't identify keeps the plain bar.

How to set it up and use it. Switch on Settings → Audio Devices → Waveform (off by default). Local files need your music mounted at /music; Qobuz and TIDAL tracks need that service signed in there, under Qobuz waveforms or TIDAL waveforms (used for waveforms only). Play something and open Now playing — the first play of a track takes a second or two to analyse.

🎵Album discovery

A screen of random albums with filtering; Album of the day — the same album on every device from 00:01 until it is played anywhere, then a new one at the next 00:01; Random Album, one tap to choose an album you haven't heard in 12 months, then Play now, Play next or Queue (its disc turns slowly and spins up while it chooses); Not played in 6 months, which starts once there are six months of your listening to go on; and Label of the week. The database learns what you've heard and resurfaces forgotten albums.

How to set it up and use it. All on Home. Under the greeting are the Random Album disc — tap it, then choose how to play what it picked — and Album of the day (★ Today). The Not played in 6 months row appears by itself once there are six months of listening to work from. Choose rows and their order under Settings → Setup → Home Screen. The Random albums heading on Home opens the full random wall; the shuffle button draws again and Filter narrows it by genre, tag or decade. Set TZ in the install command so 00:01 is your 00:01.

📚Rich library browsing

Browse by album, artist, genre, record label, decade or tag — or open the Library row on Home and scroll your entire collection, with Sort and Focus, as a grid or a list (UI Settings). Dynamic Playlists and your playlists live in the side menu, with Import at the top of the Playlists screen. Tap an artist in an album to see all their albums, and the brass ‹ at the top left takes you back. Artwork is cached on the server, so even a large library scrolls smoothly without waiting on your Core.

New in v2.0.4: a playlist's page keeps its buttons in view as you scroll, with Shuffle, a find, and an A–Z slider when sorted by Title or Artist. With native mode on, Edit playlist in its ⋯ removes a track or drags it to a new place, in Roon itself.

How to set it up and use it. Tap the Library title on Home, then use Sort (album, artist, release date, plays, last played, random) and Focus (decade, genre, source, listening) at the top. Dynamic Playlists and Playlists are in the side menu (☰); Import at the top of the Playlists screen brings a playlist in.

🔍Powerful search

Instant whole-library search by album, artist or record label. TIDAL and Qobuz are browsed through Roon, from the side menu, with Roon's own sign-ins.

How to set it up and use it. Tap the magnifier at the top of Home and type. For TIDAL and Qobuz, open them from the side menu (☰); they appear there when Roon is signed in to them.

💿Detailed album pages

Edge-to-edge artwork, track listing with per-track Play now and Queue, release date, label, every credited artist as a link, a description of the record, and the Pitchfork score with a link to the review where there is one. Below it: more by the artist, the records they appear on, and similar artists and albums from Last.fm.

New in v2.0.3: with native mode on, Versions lists every copy of the album, in your library and on TIDAL or Qobuz (tap one for its tracks and to play it), an artist's page adds Appears on, and Now playing shows Roon's signal path (tap its dot for every step). On a phone the album screen has Tracks · Credits · About · Versions tabs.

How to set it up and use it. Tap any album. Play now and Queue sit under the title, and ⋯ holds Next, Shuffle, Radio and Listen later. Tap a track for Play now / Queue, or long-press to select several. The artist's other albums and appearances need nothing; Similar artists and Similar albums appear once a free Last.fm key is saved under Settings → Setup → API Keys. Read-only — nothing is scrobbled (Roon does that).

📺Full-screen wall display

Turn a TV or tablet into a now-playing display: big artwork, artist photography, biographies, reviews and “More from” grids from your library you can tap to play, with automatic rotation or one mode pinned.

How to set it up and use it. Switch on Settings → Wall Display → Wall display (off by default) and set Rotate every. Point any browser at http://<server-ip>:3399/display, or choose ☰ → Wall display. Artist photos need a FanArt.tv key. Tap the screen for the mode buttons (Auto, Art, Photos, Bio, Review, Library); ‹ Remote and Shelf › are always in the top corners.

🏷Record label explorer

Label of the week, label logos via Discogs and FanArt.tv, label merging with undo, every release from a selected label, and labels taken from your folder names if your library is filed that way.

How to set it up and use it. Switch on Settings → Setup → Record labels — it is off by default, and nothing label-related runs until it is. Add the optional API keys for logos. Then open ☰ → Labels: long-press tiles and tap Merge to combine labels, tap “N merged” on a tile to undo.

📻Random Album Radio

When the queue finishes, the app picks another whole album and keeps the music flowing indefinitely. An album the radio played is not played by it again for six months, and among the rest it prefers ones you haven't played in 30 days.

How to set it up and use it. Switch on Settings → Audio Devices → Random album radio for the zone chosen there. Roon Radio is the alternative on the same page; turning one on turns the other off for that zone.

▶Playback integration

Play or queue albums and tracks, multi-select, seek, shuffle, repeat and volume, move the queue between zones, group zones and power devices.

How to set it up and use it. Pick your zone under Settings → Audio Devices (the zone at the top, per device), or with the speaker button on the mini player. Tap the mini player for Now playing; its device button holds the zone tools. Long-press albums on any wall to select several.

🎨Two themes, one look

Graphite and Brass (the default) and Brass light, both meeting the AA contrast standard: a greeting and today's date at the top of Home, section titles in small brass capitals, rounder covers, a mini player with a level meter and toasts that sit above it, and a ✓ in every API key box once the key has been accepted.

How to set it up and use it. Choose a theme under Settings → Setup → UI Settings → Theme and tap Apply — remembered per device. Show sample rate on artwork on the same page adds bit depth and sample rate to every tile.

🔄Automatic updates

Checks GitHub for a new release at startup and every 7 days, with one-tap updates installed in place — on every install, the ready-made Docker image and the Windows build included.

How to set it up and use it. When a release is out, a banner offers Update; tap it and the app reloads on the new version. Settings → Updates → Check for updates looks straight away.
