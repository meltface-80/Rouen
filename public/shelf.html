    Flicking through the shelf

    • Swipe moves one album
    • Swipe & hold keeps it turning, faster the further out you hold
    • Flick spins it, and it lands on an album three seconds later
    • Tap a cover at the side to bring it to the front, and the front one to turn the case over
    • Hold a track on the back of the case to choose it, then tap more to add them — play them from under the cover. With a mouse, ⌘/Ctrl-click chooses one and Shift-click a run
    • The small tabs at the edges fold the choices away to the left, and bring the queue in from the right
